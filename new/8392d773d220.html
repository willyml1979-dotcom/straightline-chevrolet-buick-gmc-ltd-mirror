Let’s take a closer look at OnStar and all of its features.

Nobody wants to have their vehicle stolen, but at least with OnStar you increase the chances of getting it back. Thanks to features like Stolen Vehicle Slowdown which can remotely slow down your GM vehicle in the event of a pursuit, or Remote Ignition Block which prevents the engine from starting up again, OnStar is definitely a thief’s worst nightmare.

OnStar can detect if your vehicle has been in an accident and send help even if you are not in a position to do so. So, OnStar is able to send help as soon as the accident happens and therefore get you out of there safely and quickly.

Perhaps the coolest feature offered by OnStar (well, aside from the Vehicle Slowdown system), is 4G LTE Wi-Fi Hotspot. This feature allows you to stay connected to the web by turning your vehicle into a Wi-Fi hotspot.
